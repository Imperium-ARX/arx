# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

We take security reports seriously and appreciate coordinated disclosure.

- **Contact:** security@imperiumos.ai
- **Scope:** the ARX platform, its provisioning and update infrastructure, and this documentation where it misstates a security property.
- **Please include:** a description of the issue, reproduction conditions where applicable, and your assessment of impact.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement of your report within **2 business days**.
- A substantive assessment within **10 business days**.
- Credit for the finding, if you want it, once a fix has shipped.

## Ground rules

- Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data belonging to any ARX customer.
- Do not run testing against production deployments serving real companies; contact us for a review arrangement instead — we support customer-led and researcher security review under NDA.
- Good-faith research conducted under this policy will not be met with legal action.

## Out of scope

- Social engineering of Imperium staff or ARX customers.
- Denial-of-service testing.
- Findings that require a compromised operating system or physical access to an unlocked, signed-in device.

<sub>This policy covers the public documentation and the platform it describes. ARX source code is not public; documentation issues can be raised as repository issues, security issues only through the contact above.</sub>
